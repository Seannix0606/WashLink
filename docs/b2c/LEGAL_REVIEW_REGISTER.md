# B2C legal review register

Open questions from the B2C work for a qualified professional to decide. Every item is **OPEN**. WashLink's code and documents make no compliance claim, and nothing here is legal advice. Only a person records an outcome.

The workforce register (`docs/workforce/LEGAL_REVIEW_REGISTER.md`) stays separate, so its item counts and tests are unchanged.

**Reviewers:** L1 to L5 and L9 go to a qualified data-privacy professional, and L5 also needs labor review. L6 to L8 go to a qualified Philippine labor-law professional.

| ID | Status | Item for review | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2C6-L1 | OPEN | **Self-deletion refused with shop booking history (RA 10173).** Refused for any customer with a `public.bookings` row in any status, including cancelled (D-91). Review against access and erasure rights and app store rules. The refusal has no time limit, and the Account tab offered as the Google Play link needs sign-in and refuses this group. If the production `amr` claim differs, every self-deletion is refused. Related: L2. | `b2ck` `delete_my_account`; `OPERATIONS.md` (Account deletion) |
| B2C6-L2 | OPEN | **Manual closure and `public.bookings` personal data (RA 10173).** Manual closure has no written procedure, owner, contact channel or response time. Deleting the user only empties `customer_id`; `customer_name`, `address`, location and notes stay. Review what must be kept, redacted or removed, and whether B2B retention applies. | `OPERATIONS.md` (Warning); `DeleteAccountSection.tsx`; `AccountScreen.tsx` |
| B2C6-L3 | OPEN | **Retained audit subject IDs (RA 10173).** `workforce_audit_events` is append-only and keeps `subject_profile_id` and `actor_id` after deletion, tied to booking IDs, plus a new `account.self_deleted` row. Review how long this stays, what users must be told, and whether a removal route must exist. | `w3b` audit table; `b2ch` `service_booking_record_transition`; `b2ck` |
| B2C6-L4 | OPEN | **Kept booking contents and cleared reasons (RA 10173).** A kept booking holds price, dates, vehicle category, reference and ratings. No retention period is set, and Philippine tax retention is not determined. Clearing all reason text on a deleted customer's bookings also removes the washer's problem reports and admin reasons, which may be needed in a dispute. Deleting a washer leaves customers' review text in place, and it may name them. | `b2ck`; `b2cf`; `OPERATIONS.md` |
| B2C6-L5 | OPEN | **Suspended washer data access and blocked deletion (RA 10173, labor).** A non-verified washer loses customer details at once, even on site mid-job (D-92), though those details were already seen. Review this access rule and what the washer may keep or must discard. A washer with a started job also can't delete their account until an admin acts, and the refusal names actions they can't take. Related: L6. | `b2ci` `my_assigned_service_bookings`, `advance_service_booking`; `b2ck` |
| B2C6-L6 | OPEN | **Suspension notice, reason and appeal (labor).** Suspension, rejection or return to `pending` removes unstarted bookings for good, with no required reason, no reason shown to the washer, no history of notes, no notice of the change and no appeal. A wash done before suspension can only be recorded as cancelled. Suspension also marks offers `declined` although the washer never responded, so no acceptance metric should count these rows. | `b2ci` `admin_review_independent_worker`; `OPERATIONS.md` (Suspending a washer) |
| B2C6-L7 | OPEN | **Overdue cancel of a done job (labor, consumer).** A customer may cancel an arrived or in-progress job from `scheduled_at + duration + 60 minutes`, with no reason, even if the work was done or is under way. The clock starts from the scheduled time, not arrival. The job drops out of earnings figures, the washer gets a generic notice, and past-job screens offer no report control. Review payment expectations, dispute handling and what each party must be told. | `b2ci` `cancel_service_booking`; `bookingStatus.ts`; D-99 |
| B2C6-L8 | OPEN | **New work rules and washer classification (labor).** The start and arrival windows (D-98, D-107), base-location lock (D-97), offer rules (D-94, D-95) and suspension (D-92) each set conditions on how washers work. Review how these bear on classifying independent washers. Nothing here states a classification. Related: L6. | `b2ci`; `b2ch`; `DECISIONS.md` |
| B2C6-L9 | OPEN | **Distance bands and radius probing (RA 10173).** Distance is stored as bands, but radius stays editable, so repeated changes could narrow down a customer's location. The band backfill can't be undone. Review whether the remaining exposure is acceptable and what should be said about it. | `b2ch`; `ARCHITECTURE.md` |
