# B2C legal review register

Open questions from the B2C work for a qualified professional to decide. Every item is **OPEN**. WashLink's code and documents make no compliance claim, and nothing here is legal advice. Only a person records an outcome.

The workforce register (`docs/workforce/LEGAL_REVIEW_REGISTER.md`) stays separate, so its item counts and tests are unchanged.

**Reviewers:** L1 to L5 and L9 go to a qualified data-privacy professional, and L5 also needs labor review. L6 to L8 go to a qualified Philippine labor-law professional. B2C7-L1 to B2C7-L6 and B2C7-L9 go to a qualified data-privacy professional, B2C7-L7 to a qualified Philippine labor-law professional, and B2C7-L8 to a qualified legal professional for customer-facing wording. B2C7-L10 to B2C7-L14 go to a qualified data-privacy professional, B2C7-L15 to both reviewers, and B2C7-L16 and B2C7-L17 to a qualified Philippine labor-law professional.
