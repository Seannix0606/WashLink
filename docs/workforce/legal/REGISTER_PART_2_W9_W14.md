# Legal review register, part 2: W9-W14

Part of the legal review register. The index, status summary, overlap map and human sign-off table are in
[`../LEGAL_REVIEW_REGISTER.md`](../LEGAL_REVIEW_REGISTER.md). This part holds the W9, W10, W11, W14 and W12 items in
the order of the earlier single-file register. The W13 items are in the index itself. W13 moved the earlier rows here
unchanged (D-75). The rows and the cross-reference paragraphs are copied verbatim, so some paragraphs still say "this register".
Every item is **OPEN** and is for review by a qualified Philippine labor-law professional (data-privacy items also by
a qualified data-privacy professional). The loop cannot resolve any item and makes no compliance claim. Nothing here
is advice. Items are never deleted; only a human records an outcome, in the index sign-off table.

Status key: OPEN = not reviewed. Source paths are relative to the repository root.
