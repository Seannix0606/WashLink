## New items

|---|---|---|
| W7-L12 | Conflict of interest in the review process (D-21). The owner who suspended or revoked a certificate is the only person who decides the worker's review, and nobody else can act until W11. Suggested W11 minimum: (a) a reviewer independent of the issuing shop (super_admin or a designated panel) can decide or escalate any review; (b) the worker can escalate an `upheld` decision; (c) a time limit for the owner to decide, after which the request escalates; (d) the reviewer sees both the owner's reason and the worker's statement, and records a reason; (e) open reviews on removed or unverified shops get a decider. Review whether the W7 interim (issuing owner only) is acceptable before production use. | `supabase/migrations/2026_10_26_w7f_certification_status_and_review_rpcs.sql:160-215` (`decide_certification_review`); DECISIONS D-21 |
| W7-L13 | Stranded suspensions. Suspension has no time limit. Reinstating (directly or through a review) needs a recorded shop verification, but suspending does not. If the issuing shop then loses verification, is deleted, or its owner account is deleted (`owner_id` set null), a suspended certificate can never be reinstated or reviewed with a reinstate outcome, and an open review stays open with no decider until W11. The worker loses the D-20 benefit with no route back except new training. | w7f lines 61, 71, 188; w7b `owner_id`/`shop_id` `on delete set null`; DECISIONS D-21, D-23 |
| W7-L14 | Retaliation risk across shops. Because D-20 recognises any shop's certificate for every certification-required opening, one owner's suspension or revocation removes a worker's access to other shops' openings. Nothing links status changes to, or guards them against, the worker declining an offer, leaving, or ending a contract or relationship (spec F: a worker's ability to decline or terminate must not be bypassed). Review whether such a suspension or revocation needs extra safeguards (independent review, cooling-off, or disclosure of the dispute). | w7f `transition_certification`; w7g:208 apply gate; w7d:60 `worker_holds_current_certification` |
| W7-L15 | Access to certification and renewal depends on shops. A worker can only reach training via an accepted application to a trainees-welcome opening (D-15). If no shop nearby posts such openings, the worker cannot get a certificate and is shut out of every certification-required opening (D-20). Renewal needs the same issuing shop, verified and willing to re-assess. The worker cannot ask for a re-assessment, and if that shop is removed or unverified, a lapsed certificate means repeating the full training. The worker UI does not explain the renewal window, that renewal depends on the issuing shop, or that an expired certificate stops counting for applications (owner screens show `renewalRuleText`; the worker card shows only the expiry date). | w7e `renew_certification` (line 217); `frontend/src/presentation/components/certifications/CertificationCard.tsx:78`; `frontend/src/presentation/utilities/certificationCopy.ts:60`; DECISIONS D-15, D-20, D-22 |
| W7-L16 | What other owners see about the issuer. The applicant section is headed "Verified certifications" and lists only the title, shop name snapshot and dates. It does not show that the issuing shop has since lost verification or left WashLink, although such a certificate still unlocks certification-required openings (D-23). Hiring owners may read "Verified" as checked by WashLink or by a government body. Review the heading and whether issuer status should be disclosed. Note the privacy trade-off with W7-L4. | `frontend/src/presentation/components/owner/applicants/ApplicantVerifiedCertificationsSection.tsx:29,55`; `supabase/migrations/2026_10_26_w7g_certification_reads_and_apply_gate.sql:136-170` |
